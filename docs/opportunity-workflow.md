# Review a lead and start an application

**Funding Radar is the shared catalog.** Its current view excludes passed,
expired and known ineligible leads for your organization. Unknown eligibility or
dates remain visible as items to verify; a lead is not a confirmed eligible grant.
Use the history filters to reconsider a passed lead or inspect a closed record.

**Needs your decision is your review queue.** It draws from current radar leads
without a prior decision or existing pipeline item. Reviewed scores are prioritized,
but free estimates and unscored leads are also available. This does not turn an
estimate into an AI score. The next item appears after you record a choice.

**Foundation Leads uses the same published opportunity records.** Its cards now
offer the same choices as Funding Radar and the dashboard:

- **Pursue:** add the lead to Capture Pipeline in Reviewing.
- **Pass:** save your organization's decision and remove the lead from default lists.
- **Pass & Share:** save a private Pass and offer neutral grant details to FinCap's
  editors under the existing review process. Your internal notes are not shared.
- **Review for Grant Factory:** open the captured lead for eligibility/date review.
  Choose **Qualify & open Grant Factory** when satisfied. Qualified or Planning
  items can open Grant Factory directly. Importing does not start paid drafting.

Existing captured items open their pipeline record rather than creating another.
Failures to save are shown, and a pipeline insert failure does not record a false
successful Pursue decision. Actions and lookups stay in the selected organization.

**Dates are checked when the list is drawn and when you act.** A saved favorable
score cannot revive an expired opportunity. A deadline today stays open for that
calendar day. Unknown dates stay unknown. Existing pipeline records and application
work remain available for review even after a deadline passes, but a new application
cannot be imported from an expired or inactive opportunity.

Use **Refresh lists — free** to load newly published opportunities and decisions.
It does not start paid scoring, paid research, or background discovery.

**Find sources matching our profile — free** searches saved approved sources.
When a source has current published grants, **Review & choose Capture / Grant Factory**
opens the actual opportunity. A general directory cannot become a grant application.
An Opportunity Assist administrator can choose **Look for current grants here — free**
for one selected source. Collection uses the existing enabled state, approved public
source URLs, page limits and review queue. Source approval and organization membership
are checked again when the job runs. No paid search or extraction provider is invoked.
Other users see why collection is unavailable rather than an unexplained dead end.

**Delete application** is available to the executive owner on unsubmitted workspace
cards in Grant Factory → Applications. It removes the workspace from active
applications while retaining its answers, documents and audit history. Open
**Deleted applications** and choose **Restore application** to recover the same
workspace. Deleted workspaces cannot generate AI work or be silently re-imported
as duplicates. Submitted/archived records remain permanent.

No database migration or new dependency is required. Budgets, paid research settings,
provider credentials, state enablement and recurring schedules are unchanged.
