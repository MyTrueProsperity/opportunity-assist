# Grant Factory: import and prepare an application

From **Capture Pipeline**, open an opportunity in **Qualified** or **Planning** and choose **Open in Grant Factory**. You can also use **Grant Factory → Import from Qualified or Planning**. The organization you are working in carries across.

OA looks for the complete public application, saves its original in your private Document Vault, and extracts its fields, limits and attachments. If the full application is behind a login, download it yourself and use **Upload / replace application source**, or paste the entire question list. A narrative worksheet can omit much of a portal application; check all fields and conditional questions.

Choose **Prepare first draft** to prepare the application together. It proposes a relevant program, leads narratives with your strongest approved proof, uses verified writing guidance from your saved research library, and reads the original funder requirements. Exact approved contact and other supported short fields can be copied. Missing facts, request totals, signatures, certifications and institutional decisions go to **Needs My Input**. The proposed program and strategy remain for your review.

Preparation also looks for published past awards. **Past awards & grantee fit → Research / add sources** lets you supply the official awards page or an annual-report excerpt. The analysis shows named recipients, disclosed amounts, funded work and comparisons with your approved facts. Sources and exact quotations are retained. A total divided by a number of awarded grants is labeled a *round average*, never a disclosed individual award. Samples are not predictions, current eligibility rules, or a substitute for costed scope.

Existing answers and edits are preserved on resume. Save your edits before preparing other answers. Each answer is saved separately, so you can reopen the application and resume after an interruption. Source replacement requires explicit confirmation when drafts exist. Reimporting the same pipeline item opens its existing application.

Review the original questions and limits, proposed program and strategy, attachments and missing inputs. Audit claims and complete final executive review before approval and export. OA does not log into a grant portal or submit an application for you.

**Usage:** Import uses bounded public reads. AI parsing, historical analysis and narrative writing use the workspace's existing AI allowance and provider configuration. This is separate from the local coding bot. Development validation uses synthetic data and stubbed AI; it makes no paid model calls. No new paid service, database migration, scheduled discovery job or source-promotion setting is required.

**Limits:** Automatic public discovery reads at most three pages per operation and has a shared time limit. Protected, scanned, oversized or ambiguous sources need an upload or manual review. Saved writing research guides preparation; it does not train the underlying model or establish applicant achievements. The application's requirements take priority over general writing advice.
