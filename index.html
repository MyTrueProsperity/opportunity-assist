FAQ

Questions buyers ask

What is Opportunity Assist?

An opportunity intelligence platform from My True Prosperity. It finds current grants and contract notices, scores how well each one fits your organization, tracks the ones you pursue, and helps you prepare the application.

Who is Opportunity Assist built for?

Workforce development organizations, youth employment providers, financial capability agencies, VITA coalitions, CRA practitioners, community-based organizations, employability programs, and small to midsize mission-driven organizations.

Is Opportunity Assist a grant database?

No. A grant database helps you identify funders. Opportunity Assist scores fit, recommends what to pursue, and manages the pursuit from first look to award.

Does it write grant applications?

Grant Factory prepares a first draft from your organization’s own documents and the funder’s requirements, and flags the evidence that is still missing. Your team reviews, edits, and submits. It does not invent facts or submit for you.

How is it different from SAM.gov, BidNet, Bonfire, Instrumentl, or Candid?

Bid portals show what is posted. Grant databases help identify funders. AI grant writers help draft language. Traditional GovCon tools support large contractors. Opportunity Assist is built for mission-driven organizations that need a practical way to find, score, track, and pursue the right opportunities.

Can it help decide whether an opportunity is worth pursuing?

Yes. The AI Fit Score rates organizational and program fit, target population, geography, applicant eligibility, capacity, funding fit, timing, past performance, and risk. Factors a listing does not cover are marked unknown, not scored as zero. You get a recommendation such as Strongly pursue or Probably pass.

Can small nonprofits use Opportunity Assist?

Yes. It is designed for small and midsize mission-driven organizations, not large government contractors.

What does it cost?

Exploring is free. Professional is $149 a month and Team is $249 a month. Enterprise pricing is set with you.

Spend less energy reacting to every posting and more on the opportunities that actually fit your mission and capacity.
